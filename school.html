Director's Message

At Lawrence High School Kamwenge, we believe that education extends far beyond textbooks. We foster an environment that builds strong moral values, academic rigor, and practical vocational talent.

Our students are equipped to succeed in national UNEB examinations while simultaneously acquiring real-world skills that prepare them for career independence.
